What the evidence supports

The Decision-Relevant Context Probe maintained its directional advantage across random seeds, review capacities, scenario mixtures, Context Probe friction assumptions, and intervention-economics assumptions.

This supports the canonical AegisPay prototype policy.

What this does not establish

Responsible interpretation is part of the result.

Synthetic benchmark
  • Not production validation or measured fraud reduction.
  • Not real customer-response behavior or analyst workload evidence.
  • Not real upay transaction prevalence or economics.
  • Intervention effectiveness, friction, and operations costs are assumed/modelled.
  • No ML model is part of the current validated pipeline.
